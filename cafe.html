    Prashna — questions people ask

    What do I get for my contribution?

    40% of it back as café credit when the doors open (e.g. ₹2,500 → ₹1,000), your name on the public ledger if you want it, a vote on what goes inside, the right to apply to run it — and a café that exists because of you. You do not get equity, profit, interest or ownership.

    How does the café credit work?

    It's a closed-loop credit usable only at the café: up to 50% of any bill, valid 12 months from opening day, non-transferable, never cashable. Sign in with the email you paid with and it shows under "My café credit". GST on what you order is included at the counter like any customer.

    Is there a refund?

    Not for change of mind. But if the café doesn't open within 365 days of the target being reached, or the target isn't reached within 365 days of the first contribution, every contribution is refunded within 15 days, net of the payment-gateway charge (about 2–3%, which Razorpay and the banks keep — we don't). Until the target is hit the money sits untouched, so it's always there to give back.

    Who picks the person who runs the café?

    A panel whose names and scoring criteria are published before selection. Applicants are shortlisted on the written plan and invited to a presentation day. The selection is on merit — it is not a draw, and contributing more does not help.

    Does the selected person own the café?

    No. The café is owned by the Asa Kela Pahije entity that received the funds. The selected person operates it under a partnership agreement whose key terms (duration, revenue share, responsibilities, exit) are published.

    Who holds the money?

    Our Dreamwork (a sole proprietorship of Tejas Salvi), in a separate account used only for the café. Nothing is spent before the target is reached. Every rupee received and every rupee spent appears in the public ledger and expense table.

    What if the café can't open?

    That is a real risk — rent, licences and permissions in Mumbai are not simple. The policy for that case will be printed in the terms before any money is taken. Until it is, payments stay closed.

    Is "₹1 crore, 4 cafés" real?

    No — it's arithmetic. The reel got about 40 lakh views; if one in 200 viewers gave an average of ₹500, that would be ₹1 crore, which funds four cafés at ₹25 lakh each. The only real number is the live counter above.

    How do you stop duplicate votes?

    You sign in (Google or email link) and the database allows one ballot per account per round, enforced on the server. Someone with many email accounts could still vote more than once — so results are advisory and the team retains judgement.

    What do you do with my data?

    Email is used to confirm submissions, stop duplicate votes, match applications to contributions and tell you when things happen. It is not published, sold or shared. Card details never touch our site — payment happens on Razorpay's page.
